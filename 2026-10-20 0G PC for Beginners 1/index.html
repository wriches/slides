AI BASICS  ·  PICK THE RIGHT TOOL

The terminal is where AI has the most power

Chat

Quick questions and drafts. It only knows what you paste in.

CLI agent

Works on your machine. Reads files, runs commands, edits docs and code. Anything you can do in a terminal.

Specialised tools

Design, slides, images and video. A tool built for one job still beats a general agent there.

Default to the CLI for real work. Switch when the output is visual.

AI BASICS  ·  GIVE IT EVERYTHING

Low entropy in, low quality out

Thin
write a tweet announcing GLM 5.3 on PC

Generic. Could be any company, any model.

Rich
write a tweet announcing GLM 5.3 on PC
+ release notes PDF
+ the pricing page
+ the Slack thread on our angle
+ three past tweets we liked

Specific, on-brand, with the right numbers.

Don't tidy it, dump it. The model is great at sorting a mess. It can't invent what you didn't give it.

AI BASICS  ·  ONE CHUNK AT A TIME

Ask for one piece, review it, then move on

AskThe landing page
→
ReviewRead it, try it, fix it
→
NextThe sign-up form

"Build the whole event site" gives you more than you can check, so you end up accepting it unread. Small pieces are easy to review and easy to undo.

AI BASICS  ·  NEVER ACCEPT A MISS

Every hand edit is a rule you haven't written

It gets something wrong
→
Say what you wanted, and why
→
Ask it to save that as a rule
→
Next time it's right
You keep opening emails with pleasantries. I want the ask in the first line. Add that to my CLAUDE.md with an example.
AI BASICS  ·  WRITE RULES THAT WORK

Show it the target, and say why

Restrictions
Don't be verbose.
Don't use jargon.
Don't use so many bullet points.

Says what's wrong, not what good looks like.

An example, with the reason
Write like a message to a busy colleague: the ask first, then two plain sentences. They read it on their phone.
e.g. "Can you check the GLM row on the pricing page today? Both prices changed on Friday."

The reason covers the cases you didn't list.

AI BASICS  ·  THE LAST 10%

The first 90% is fast. The last 10% is the work.

First 90%Minutes. It feels done.
Last 10%Edge cases, polish, checking it's right

Enjoy the speed. Don't mistake a fast draft for a finished job.

AI BASICS  ·  AMPLIFY YOUR EXPERTISE

Amplify your expertise, don't fake someone else's

In your field

AI makes you faster and better. You can tell good from okay.

Outside it

AI gets you to okay, and you can't see what's missing. Use it to shorten the loop: a sharper brief, a first draft, better questions. Then hand it to the expert.

A marketer with AI beats an engineer with AI at marketing.

CLAUDE CODE ON PC  ·  YOUR WORKSPACE

One folder for everything, one folder per project

~/.claude/CLAUDE.md your rules, every session ~/AI/ start Claude here ├─ CLAUDE.md map of your projects ├─ team-intro/ │ └─ CLAUDE.md this project's rules └─ launch-post/
  • CLAUDE.md is a plain text file of rules. Claude Code reads it every session.
  • New idea? Ask for a new folder. Everything starts on your laptop.
  • Worth keeping? Ask it to push the folder to GitHub.
